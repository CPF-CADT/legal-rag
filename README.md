# LegalRAG — Legal Document Search & Question-Answering System

LegalRAG is a **legal information retrieval and AI-powered question-answering system** designed to help users search, explore, and understand Cambodian laws and legal documents.

## Key Features

### 1. Legal Document Search

- Search for relevant laws and legal articles using keywords.
- Retrieve legal articles related to a user's query.
- Access references and links to original legal documents.

### 2. AI Question Answering

- Ask questions about Cambodian laws in natural language.
- Generate context-aware answers using retrieved legal information.
- Provide source references to help users verify AI-generated answers.

## Project Objectives

- Improve accessibility to Cambodian legal information.
- Help users quickly locate relevant laws and legal articles.
- Reduce the time required to manually search legal documents.
- Provide AI-assisted explanations grounded in legal sources.

## Dataset

Legal documents are sourced from the **National Committee for Sub-National Democratic Development (NCDD) Digital Library**.

**Source:** <https://library.ncdd.gov.kh>
